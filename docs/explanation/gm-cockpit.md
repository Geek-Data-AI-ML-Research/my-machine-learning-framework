# GM cockpit

Why the studio is six modes (BRAIN, GOALS, LAB, KAGGLE, OPS, TRUST) instead of a twelve-tab bar: 88 characters is the board width. Sub-views open from `:` / Cmd+K. Motion is reserved for an active train strip (SSE `train_strip`), not an idle animation.

Canonical folds, ADV_AUC, and fold SNR badges live on the Overview header. Ensemble `mode=auto` is the honesty check (nested CV, not the in-sample blend). Night-Shift digest is a morning board with human CTAs; it never submits to Kaggle.
